Product Guide

Know what you're buying

A short introduction to what we stock and how people love to use it.

🌰

Walnuts

Rich, buttery and full of omega-3. Enjoy as a snack, in baked goods, salads and traditional sweets.

Store: cool, dry place

Pistachios

Naturally sweet green kernels, sold raw or roasted & salted. Perfect for desserts, ice cream and snacking.

Roasted · Raw · Shelled

Almonds

Crunchy and mild, high in protein and vitamin E. Available whole, sliced or blanched for cooking.

Great for sweets & milk

Raisins

Sun-dried grapes with natural sweetness, used in rice dishes, desserts and breakfast bowls.

Golden · Black · Green

Rice

Long-grain basmati for biryani and machboos, plus everyday varieties. Aged grains cook fluffy and fragrant.
